Who We Are

Empower Ministry Group is a non-profit investment company dedicated to revitalizing underserved neighborhoods. We believe that true empowerment comes from economic stability and community ownership.

Community First

Every investment decision is vetted by a board of local leaders.

Sustainable Growth

Our Journey

A timeline of our growth and impact.

2018

The Foundation

Empower Ministry Group was founded by a coalition of 5 local churches seeking to pool resources for greater impact.

2020

First Major Acquisition

Purchased the "Westside Complex," preserving 12 small businesses during the economic downturn.

2022

Non-Profit Status Granted

Officially recognized as a 501(c)(3), allowing us to expand our grant-writing capabilities and accept tax-deductible donations.

Present

Regional Expansion

Currently managing over $5M in community assets and expanding into neighboring counties.
