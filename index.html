Our Journey

A timeline of our growth and impact.

2002-2005

The Beginning

In 2002, Empower Ministry began as a grassroots initiative founded by Julius Nam, Jonathan Park, and Robin Park. Its mission was to equip and train youth and young adults for church ministry, focusing especially on the Korean-American Adventist community. The founders launched the Empower Leadership Conference, a five- to seven-day annual event in Southern California, gathering young leaders for worship, leadership development, and ministry networking. These early gatherings became a spiritual training ground for emerging pastors, student leaders, and volunteers across North America.

2006-2011

Incorporation and Expansion

To strengthen its organizational foundation, Empower Ministry Group, Inc. was formally incorporated in 2006by Chin H. Kim as a California Nonprofit Religious Corporation, building on the founders’ original mission. Incorporation allowed EMG to expand its administrative capacity, manage donations, and provide broader ministry oversight. During this period, Empower Leadership Conferences continued annually, drawing attendees from multiple states and producing a new generation of trained youth and young adult leaders.

2012-2018

Ministry Development

EMG emerged as a supporting hub for Adventist youth and ministry partnerships, providing leadership resources, retreats, and conferences. EMG worked jointly with the Southeastern California Conference, extending its leadership and training reach. In May 2018, EMG established a scholarship program for graduate students at Loma Linda University, investing in future ministry and professional leaders. The organization also expanded its fiscal sponsorships, partnering with ministries such as KAYAMM (Korean American Youth Adventist Missionary Movement) and other church-based initiatives to foster mission engagement and discipleship.

2019-Present

Expanded Reach

In 2019, EMG began organizing and supporting the West Coast Korean Campmeeting, an annual gathering uniting Korean-American Adventists from across North America. In 2020, EMG launched two DBAs: Project Mist, in partnership with Yorba Linda Church, focusing on creative ministry, worship, and digital outreach, and Free Church Resources, providing free ministry materials, seminars, and training tools to churches and ministry teams. In 2023, EMG launched POP Ministry (Power of Prayer Ministry) to deepen spiritual revival and intercessory prayer across congregations, emphasizing spiritual formation and the power of collective prayer. In 2024, EMG expanded to support the East Coast Korean Campmeeting, fostering faith and leadership growth within the Korean Adventist community. In 2026, EMG recruited Dr. D. David Kim and Paul Jeon to join Dr. Chin H. Kim to its board to continue Empowering Ministries for Possibilities.
